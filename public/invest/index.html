      Bonds Demo data

      US Treasury Bonds

      Loans to the government. Generally lower risk, with interest paid on a schedule.

      • Issuer: government
      • Default risk: relatively low
      • Price still moves with interest rates

      Corporate Bonds

      Loans to companies. Usually pay more interest, with more credit risk.

      • Issuer: company
      • Default risk: higher than government debt
      • Yield reflects the issuer’s credit quality
